► overview

The internet has made collaboration more accessible than ever, creating new opportunities for East Asian artists and underground music scenes to intersect, cross-pollinate, and blossom.

Conflux Corps is a music festival designed for young adults drawn to internet music, cross-cultural works, and contemporary East Asian artistry, offering a space where young East Asians can feel culturally represented and be a part of something vibrant and collective.

The festival concept was inspired by the energy of live music spaces and their ability to bring people together through shared interests, self-expression and cultural connection.

At the time, I was particularly interested in the evolving East Asian music scene, where artists from different countries were collaborating across languages and cultures.

Check out Tesla↗ by Kegøn, featuring artists Billionhappy & Effie.

I saw this as an exciting cultural moment worth capturing, and wanted to create a festival that celebrated this growing sense of connection through music.
